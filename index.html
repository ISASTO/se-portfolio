About

I like building software I can actually use.

I’m studying software engineering at the University of Minnesota Crookston. Most of my projects begin with something close to home: a person who needs a website, a tool I wish existed, or a task I am tired of doing by hand.

I enjoy getting a rough idea working, then staying with it. That usually means testing on real devices, fixing awkward mobile behavior, tracing edge cases, and writing tools that make the next update easier.
