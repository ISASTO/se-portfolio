About

I stay with projects after the first version works.

I’m a software engineering student at the University of Minnesota Crookston, studying online from Minneapolis and graduating in spring 2028. Most of my projects begin close to home: a person who needs a website, a tool I wish existed, or a task I am tired of doing by hand.

What interests me most comes next. I test with real people and real devices, fix the awkward cases, and simplify the next update. That has led me from static pages to backend services, from a simulator to a physical watch, and from a manual publishing checklist to a reusable command-line tool.
